# CSC 321 – Introduction to Artificial Intelligence

This repository contains my coursework for **CSC 321: Introduction to Artificial Intelligence**.

The coursework explores fundamental AI concepts, problem-solving techniques, knowledge representation, logical reasoning, probabilistic methods, and introductory machine learning.

## Topics Covered

- **Solving Problems by Searching** – Problem formulation and search algorithms
- **Informed Search and Exploration** – Heuristic search strategies and exploration techniques
- **Logic and Inference** – Logical reasoning and inference systems
- **Knowledge Representation** – Representing facts, knowledge, and relationships
- **Statistical and Probabilistic Methods** – Probability and reasoning under uncertainty
- **Basics of Machine Learning** – Foundational machine learning concepts and algorithms

## Learning Objectives

Through this coursework, I explore AI agents, search algorithms, constraint satisfaction problems (CSPs), logical inference, probabilistic reasoning, and fundamental machine learning techniques.

The assignments and exercises focus on applying theoretical concepts to practical problems and understanding different approaches to building intelligent systems.

## Textbook

*Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach* (4th Edition)  
Stuart Russell and Peter Norvig
